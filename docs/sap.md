## Agile, Smart and Intelligent Technologies

SAP enables businesses of all sizes and in all industries to operate profitably, adapt continuously, and realize strategic goals. Centric Limited is one of the leading SAP East Africa partners in delivering SAP implementations and support services for SAP solutions.

## What is SAP S/4HANA?

SAP S/4HANA is an intelligent ERP, enabling companies to deliver the ultimate customer experience through the power of the intelligent enterprise. It transforms business processes with intelligent automation and runs on SAP HANA — a market-leading in-memory database offering real-time processing speeds and a dramatically simplified data model.

Two deployment options:
- **SAP S/4HANA (On-Premise & Partner Cloud)** — complete control for sophisticated, customer-specific business processes across Core ERP, Intelligent Finance and Risk, and Digital Supply Chain.
- **SAP S/4HANA Cloud** — the industry capabilities companies require with the cloud benefits customers want.

Features & benefits: 50% reduction in data footprint; 100x faster reporting; embedded AI; real-time advanced analytics; streamlined data display; industry-specific business processes; consumer-grade UX across devices; flexible deployment options.

## What is SAP Business One?

An affordable, comprehensive solution for managing an entire small business — sales, customer relationships, financials, and operations. Key functionalities: Accounting & Financials, Sales & Customers (opportunity management, customer service), Purchasing, Inventory & Distribution, Financial project management, MRP, Production Management, Reporting & Administration, built-in workflows/alerts/approvals.

## What is SAP Analytics Cloud?

Combines business intelligence, augmented analytics, predictive analytics, and planning in one cloud environment — the analytics layer of SAP's Business Technology Platform. AI/ML/NLP-powered augmented analytics are built into BI and planning workflows.

## SAP Ariba

E-procurement and supply-chain cloud solutions tying together 30+ processes from source to pay, integrated with SAP S/4HANA for cost accounting, payments, and spend analysis. Includes Solutions for Direct Spend, Supply Chain Collaboration, Strategic Sourcing, Supply Base Optimization, guided buying, and Spend Analysis (enriched with Dun & Bradstreet data).
