## What it does
Centric's queue management solutions streamline customer flow, on-premises ordering, and staff performance to deliver a better client experience, for healthcare, public sector, finance, and retail organizations.

## Typical capabilities
- Ticket/ virtual queueing at branches and service points
- Digital signage and audio call-forward for ticket numbers
- SMS/mobile queue-position notifications so customers don't have to wait in a physical line
- Branch and counter performance reporting (wait times, service times, staff throughput)
- Appointment/pre-booking integration to reduce walk-in queue pressure

## Why it matters for these industries
- **Healthcare** — shorter, calmer waiting rooms and fairer patient call order.
- **Public sector** — predictable, auditable service at high-volume citizen service counters.
- **Finance** — faster branch throughput and reduced in-branch congestion.
- **Retail** — smoother in-store service during peak periods.
