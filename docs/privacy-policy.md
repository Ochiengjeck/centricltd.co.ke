# Privacy Policy — `/privacy-policy/` (rewritten)

The live version at https://centricltd.co.ke/privacy-policy/ references the wrong domain (`www.centriclimited.co.ke`) and is mostly generic WordPress boilerplate (cookie/comment/analytics language that doesn't match what this site actually does, no mention of the live-chat widget, no clear statement that the contact form has no backend). Rewritten below to be accurate to the actual new site. This is the content used by `src/data/privacyPolicy.js` / `PrivacyPolicyPage`.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

## Who we are
This policy covers `centricltd.co.ke`, operated by Centric Limited, Westpark Towers, Mpesi Lane Off Parklands Road, Nairobi, Kenya. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@centricltd.co.ke.

## Contact form
The "Contact Us" page lets you send us a message. This form has **no server-side backend** — when you submit it, your browser opens a pre-filled email to sales@centricltd.co.ke through your own email client. Centric does not receive, log, or store anything you type into the form until you actually send that email yourself; it is not transmitted to any Centric server first.

## Live chat widget
This site includes a live-chat widget (hosted at app.ddprojects.ae) so you can message us directly. If you use it:
- The widget uses **Pusher** to deliver chat messages in real time.
- The widget uses **Cloudflare Turnstile** to help distinguish real visitors from bots before a chat can be started.
- Any message you send through the widget is handled by these third-party providers and by Centric's chat operator in order to respond to you. We do not control what Pusher or Cloudflare Turnstile themselves log as part of providing that service — see their own privacy policies for details.

## Cookies and local storage
The core site (pages, navigation) does not set its own cookies or use browser storage. The live-chat widget may set its own cookies/local storage to keep your chat session working across page loads; this is controlled by the widget provider, not by Centric's own code.

## What we don't do
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use the contact form or chat widget content for advertising or share it with anyone outside of responding to your enquiry.

## Changes to this policy
If how this site collects or uses data changes (for example, if a server-side contact form or analytics is added later), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
