# SAP S/4HANA (`/sap-s4hana/`)

Source: https://centricltd.co.ke/sap-s4hana/ (fetched 2026-10-16)

## Intelligent ERP, enabling companies to deliver
An intelligent ERP makes it possible for companies to adapt and scale with the agility needed to stay at the forefront of ever-shifting markets.

## CRM & Sales and Distribution
Pre-sale research and offers; order processing and automatic demand/delivery creation; promotions; supply/delivery documentation; invoicing/credit/debit notes; sales-process accompanying documents; stock control (order blocking); contract-specific pricing/discount changes during invoicing.

## Logistics (Purchasing & Stores)
Supplier selection; supply contracts; stock processing; batch/serial number management; warehouse delivery/shipping; stock monitoring; material movements; supplier returns; inventory.

## Production Management
Planning & production management module ties customer orders, supply contracts, and material-resource planning into full logistics management. Tracks: finished-product/scrap/in-progress quantities, operation/order costs, resource usage, running time, start/end of process, responsible staff, planned/unplanned movements, deviation reasons. Supports: production-order updates, automatic raw-material insertion, automatic goods receipt, capacity release, cost/availability updates.

## Customer Service
SAP CS improves customer relations: customer database, staff responsible for relationships, order history, warranty data (conditions/dates/coverage). Integrated with all other SAP modules.

## Project Management
Integrated with other SAP modules for instant access to stock, delivery pricing/terms, payment terms, resource scheduling, and project risk data. Benefits: accurate planning, risk management, shorter execution time, cost control, higher project success rate, budget-vs-actual analysis, resource coordination.

## Finance
Fully integrated financial and management accounting, double-entry bookkeeping per national/international standards. Main components: General Ledger, Accounts Payable/Receivable, Asset Accounting, Bank Accounting, financial reports, cost centers, internal orders, profitability analysis, budget planning/control, margin analysis.

## Human Resources Management
A full HCM suite for personnel administration, working-time management, payroll generation with automatic posting to finance, and talent/performance evaluation at individual and team level. Key features: organizational management, personnel administration, recruitment, talent management, compensation & benefits, time management, learning management, reporting/analytics, payroll.

## Methodology for Deployment
Implemented using SAP Activate, SAP's internationally standardized methodology for fast, efficient rollouts, in four phases:
1. **Prepare** — project kickoff, planning, and system provisioning.
2. **Explore** — validate business requirements against standard SAP processes.
3. **Realize** — build and test the solution iteratively.
4. **Deploy** — final testing, data migration, cutover, and go-live.
