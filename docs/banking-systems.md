# Banking Systems (`/banking-systems/`)

Source: https://centricltd.co.ke/banking-systems/ (fetched 2026-10-16) — Fern Software partnership

Fern Software has 41 years of experience providing banking systems for banks, microfinance institutions, and loan organizations worldwide. Centric Limited has partnered with Fern Software to bring financial institutions online — managing loan portfolios, tracking client and business transactions, within tight security.

## Abacus
A modern, fully integrated core banking solution.
Benefits: multi-lingual by design; client-centric customer management; 100+ built-in reports plus BizNet financial reporting; strong user security/encryption; omnichannel access anywhere, anytime; flexible, combinable product-definition parameters for community banking; real-time integration with leading management-reporting tools.
Features: ATM Management, Compliance Tracking, Corporate Banking, Credit Card Management, Multi-Branch, Online Banking, Private Banking, Retail Banking, Risk Management, Transaction Monitoring.

## Faasbank
A flexible loan-management platform for tracking client/business transactions across varied credit-service scenarios.
Benefits: build sustainable client relationships; create innovative client-centric products/services; integrate with third-party software/services; rich visual reporting via FaaSBank BI dashboards.
Features: Accounting Management, Amortization Schedule, Loan Processing, Borrower Management, Business Loans, Collateral Tracking, Collections Management, Document Management.

## Baytree
A standard enterprise platform merging borrower management with marketing and service, operable via a smartphone app (iOS and Android).

### Baytree mobile banking features
- Send and receive texts with borrowers
- Digital signature verification
- Web-based — compatible with Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and Chrome OS
- Scales to 500,000+ concurrent users
- Mobile app for iOS and Android
- Powerful interactive reports
- Automated workflows and processes
- Custom fields and layout

## Mobile Apps
Custom mobile banking apps (iOS and Android) that help borrowers complete applications faster and give institutions an easier way to communicate with clients.
Main characteristics: all Member Access features and more; native iOS/Android apps; sign-in via facial recognition, fingerprint, or PIN; secure push-notification messaging for instant replies.
