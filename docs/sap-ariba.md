# SAP Ariba (`/sap-ariba/`)

Source: https://centricltd.co.ke/sap-ariba/ (fetched 2026-10-16)

SAP Ariba intelligent spend management ties together 30+ processes with comprehensive transaction data for efficiency, accuracy, and control from source to pay. Integrates with SAP S/4HANA for cost accounting, payments, and in-depth spend analysis.

## Solutions for Direct Spend
End-to-end digital procurement integrated with product design/R&D through manufacturing execution and delivery; integrates with PLM, ERP, and SAP Integrated Business Planning.
- **Faster time to market** — integrate R&D/design with sourcing, procurement, supply chain.
- **Quick, accurate sourcing** — source multi-level BOMs on one platform.
- **Effective supplier collaboration** — identify supply shortages before they occur.
- **Better product cost management** — minimize contract leakage, realize negotiated savings.
- **Stronger compliance** — tie supplier qualification to sourcing.
- **Complete supply chain visibility** — collaborate across supplier tiers, reduce inventory, increase fill rates.

Sub-solutions: **Supply Chain Collaboration** (work with direct-materials suppliers, improve visibility, minimize risk), **Strategic Sourcing** (qualified suppliers, faster sourcing cycles, best-value agreements), **Supply Base Optimization** (predictive analytics/optimization/simulation for savings targets and award decisions).

## Procurement Solutions (Guided Buying)
Included free with SAP Ariba Buying / Buying and Invoicing. Helps employees buy from preferred suppliers quickly, comply with purchasing policy, and collaborate with suppliers/category experts without needing to memorize the rules first.

## Spend Analysis
Gathers spend data from everywhere, classifies it to company/industry standards, and enriches it with Dun & Bradstreet market intelligence. Shows spend by supplier family, where savings leak/prices vary/purchasing overlaps, and benchmarks against industry peers — plus supplier diversity/sustainability insight.
