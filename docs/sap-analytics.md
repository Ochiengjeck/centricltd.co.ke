# SAP Analytics Cloud (`/sap-analytics/`)

Source: https://centricltd.co.ke/sap-analytics/ (fetched 2026-10-16)

## What is SAP Analytics Cloud?
Combines BI, augmented and predictive analytics, and planning into one cloud environment — the analytics layer of SAP's Business Technology Platform. AI/ML/NLP-powered augmented analytics are built into BI and planning workflows.

Key capabilities: discover/analyse/plan/predict in one experience across devices; end-to-end decisions with data management and analytics together; scales across strategic, operational, and tactical decision types.

## Business Intelligence
Explore data across the organization and deliver insights at the point of decision with self-service analytics. Gain instant insights in context of business processes; analyse/predict/plan/execute within the business process; built-in collaboration for engagement and accountability.

## Planning
Link and create financial and operational plans seamlessly in one solution. Plan, analyse, and simulate directly within the enterprise solution; collaborate on plans in context; predictive planning uncovers top performance influencers via forecasting and ML.

## Augmented Analytics
Make smarter, faster decisions with AI-driven insights: ask questions conversationally and get natural-language answers; automated ML detects KPI drivers and unknown relationships in data; predict outcomes and generate forecasts with confidence.
