# SAP Business One (`/sap-business-one/`)

Source: https://centricltd.co.ke/sap-business-one/ (fetched 2026-10-16)

## Run your business with a digitized ERP solution
Gain competitive advantage by implementing robust business processes that adapt to market changes and anticipate trends. Connect business functions so the entire business runs more smoothly, with natural cross-departmental collaboration. Get easy access to data through analytics and reports to make real-time, informed decisions.

Digital transformation is built on: Analytics, Big Data, Cloud Technologies, Mobile Technologies — giving companies the ability to change business models and create new products/services in the digital economy.

## Financial Management
Improve margins, reduce errors, and drive more profitable decisions with a complete set of tools for streamlined financial operations.
- **Accounting** — automate journal entries, accounts receivable/payable handling.
- **Controlling** — manage cash flow, track fixed assets, control budgets, monitor project costs.
- **Fixed asset management** — streamlined asset management with virtual functions.
- **Banking and reconciliation** — faster reconciliations, bank statements, payments.
- **Financial reporting and analysis** — standard or customized reports from real-time data.

## Sales & Customer Management
Oversee the entire sales process and customer lifecycle — from initial contact to final sale, after-sales service, and support.
- **Sales and opportunity management** — track leads and opportunities through quotes, sales orders, and the pipeline to close.
- **Marketing campaign management** — create, manage, and analyze marketing activities.
- **Customer management** — centralized critical customer data.
- **Service management** — warranty contracts and service agreements.
- **Reporting and analysis** — detailed sales-process reports from templates.
- **Mobile sales** — manage information across mobile devices.

## Purchasing & Inventory
Optimize purchasing practices and control costs across the complete order-to-pay cycle.
- **Procurement** — requisition requests, purchase orders, goods receipts, returns.
- **Master data management** — centralized purchasing data.
- **Warehouse and accounting integration** — real-time sync of goods receipts and inventory.
- **Accounts payable** — invoices, cancellations, credit memos with PO reference.
- **Reporting** — integrated, real-time reports across devices.
